# Contexto: La Cruz Market - Lesson 1
## Scenario: Buying Indian Groceries & Local Produce

---

## CONTEXT
**Person:** Jasmine (expat from India, 2 years in Querétaro)  
**Location:** Mercado La Cruz (main market in Querétaro)  
**Situation:** Shopping for Indian spices + local vegetables + fish  
**Goal:** Learn haggling, asking for quality, negotiating prices, finding specific items  
**Time:** Saturday morning (busy market day)

---

## LESSON 1: MARKET INTRODUCTION & HAGGLING

### Dialogue 1: Finding the Spice Vendor
```
Jasmine: "Hola, buenos días. ¿Dónde puedo encontrar cúrcuma?"
ENGLISH: "Hi, good morning. Where can I find turmeric?"

Vendedor: "Ah, cúrcuma. Tengo muy buena, mira."
ENGLISH: "Ah, turmeric. I have very good quality, look."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto cuesta el kilo?"
ENGLISH: "How much is it per kilo?"

Vendedor: "250 pesos el kilo."
ENGLISH: "250 pesos per kilo."

Jasmine: "Mmm, es un poco caro. ¿Cuál es el mejor precio?"
ENGLISH: "Hmm, it's a bit expensive. What's your best price?"

Vendedor: "Mira, para ti, 220 pesos. Está muy fresca."
ENGLISH: "Look, for you, 220 pesos. It's very fresh."

Jasmine: "Está bien. Dame medio kilo."
ENGLISH: "Okay. Give me half a kilo."

Vendedor: "Perfecto. ¿Qué más necesitas?"
ENGLISH: "Perfect. What else do you need?"
```

### Dialogue 2: Buying Fresh Fish
```
Jasmine: "¿Tienes pescado fresco?"
ENGLISH: "Do you have fresh fish?"

Vendedor de Pescado: "Sí, tengo tilapia, mojarra y trucha. ¿Cuál prefieres?"
ENGLISH: "Yes, I have tilapia, bass, and trout. Which do you prefer?"

Jasmine: "¿Cuál es más fresco?"
ENGLISH: "Which one is fresher?"

Vendedor: "La tilapia llegó esta mañana. Muy fresca."
ENGLISH: "The tilapia arrived this morning. Very fresh."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto cuesta?"
ENGLISH: "How much does it cost?"

Vendedor: "85 pesos el kilo."
ENGLISH: "85 pesos per kilo."

Jasmine: "¿Es el mejor precio? Ayer vi a 75."
ENGLISH: "Is that your best price? I saw it for 75 yesterday."

Vendedor: "Ese pescado no era fresco como este. Este es de hoy. Te lo dejo en 80."
ENGLISH: "That fish wasn't as fresh as this. This is from today. I'll give it to you for 80."

Jasmine: "Está bien. Dame un kilo, por favor."
ENGLISH: "Okay. Give me one kilo, please."
```

### Dialogue 3: Negotiating Vegetables
```
Jasmine: "¿Qué vegetales frescos tienes hoy?"
ENGLISH: "What fresh vegetables do you have today?"

Vendedora: "Tengo chiles rojos, tomates, cebollas, cilantro, todo fresco."
ENGLISH: "I have red chillies, tomatoes, onions, cilantro, all fresh."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto por esto?" (pointing at red chillies)
ENGLISH: "How much for this?"

Vendedora: "Los chiles rojos están a 30 pesos el kilo. Están muy frescos, recién traídos."
ENGLISH: "Red chillies are 30 pesos per kilo. Very fresh, just arrived."

Jasmine: "¿Puedo probar uno?" (testing freshness by pressing gently)
ENGLISH: "Can I check one?"

Vendedora: "Claro, claro. ¿Ves? Firme, fresco."
ENGLISH: "Of course. See? Firm, fresh."

Jasmine: "Bueno. Dame 250 gramos de chiles y 2 kilos de tomates."
ENGLISH: "Okay. Give me 250 grams of chillies and 2 kilos of tomatoes."

Vendedora: "Perfecto. ¿Algo más?"
ENGLISH: "Perfect. Anything else?"

Jasmine: "¿Tienes rábanos? Para mi esposo le encantan."
ENGLISH: "Do you have radishes? My husband loves them."

Vendedora: "Sí, aquí están. Muy fresco."
ENGLISH: "Yes, here they are. Very fresh."
```

### Dialogue 4: Finding Indian Spices (Dialogue with shop owner)
```
Jasmine: "Disculpe, ¿dónde encontramos harina de garbanzo?"
ENGLISH: "Excuse me, where can I find gram flour?"

Vendedor de Especias: "¿Harina de garbanzo? Tengo, pero es importada. Es un poco cara."
ENGLISH: "Gram flour? I have it, but it's imported. A bit expensive."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto cuesta?"
ENGLISH: "How much is it?"

Vendedor: "150 pesos el kilo. Es de muy buena calidad."
ENGLISH: "150 pesos per kilo. Very good quality."

Jasmine: "¿Tienes methi también? (fenugreek)"
ENGLISH: "Do you have fenugreek too?"

Vendedor: "Sí, claro. ¿Seco o fresco?"
ENGLISH: "Yes, of course. Dried or fresh?"

Jasmine: "Seco, por favor."
ENGLISH: "Dried, please."

Vendedor: "Methi seco, 80 pesos. ¿Qué más necesitas? ¿Rava? ¿Jaggery?"
ENGLISH: "Dried fenugreek, 80 pesos. What else do you need? Semolina? Jaggery?"

Jasmine: "Sí, rava. ¿Cuánto?"
ENGLISH: "Yes, semolina. How much?"

Vendedor: "Rava, 110 pesos el kilo. Y jaggery, 90 pesos."
ENGLISH: "Semolina, 110 pesos per kilo. And jaggery, 90 pesos."

Jasmine: "Está bien. Dame medio kilo de cada uno."
ENGLISH: "Okay. Give me half a kilo of each."
```

## LOCATION INFO: LA CRUZ MARKET

### Market Overview
- **Location:** Central market area, Querétaro
- **Best time:** Saturday mornings (freshest produce)
- **Sections:** Vegetables, fish, spices, Indian items, flowers, general groceries
- **Payment:** Cash preferred (small change helpful)
- **Language:** Spanish spoken, vendors familiar with expats
- **Accessibility:** Bus route available, walking distance from city center

### Key Vendor Types
| Vendor | Speciality | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-------|
| Vendedor de Pescado | Fish/Seafood | Usually near entrance, ice keeps items fresh |
| Vendedora de Verduras | Vegetables/Produce | Multiple stalls, compare prices |
| Vendedor de Especias | Spices/Dry goods | Stocks Indian items, imports available |
| Vendedor General | Groceries/Bulk items | Flour, oils, condiments |

## CULTURAL NOTES FOR EXPATS

### Haggling is NORMAL
- Expect to negotiate, especially for bulk purchases
- Vendors expect this; it's not rude
- Start at 80-90% of asking price
- Mexican vendors respect when you know your values/prices

### Quality Checking
- It's acceptable to touch/press produce
- Press fish gently to check firmness
- Ask vendor "¿Es de hoy?" (Is it from today?)
- "Recién traído" = just arrived (freshest)

### Market Etiquette
- Go early (8-10 AM) for freshest items
- Use Spanish; vendors appreciate effort
- Cash preferred; bring small bills (coins helpful)
- Plastic bags available but bring your own reusable bags
- Always say "buenos días" and "gracias"

### Indian Items Availability
- Most spices available at main spice vendors
- Gram flour, rava, jaggery = commonly stocked
- Fenugreek (methi/dried) = reliable source
- Turmeric, cumin, sesame seeds = always available
- Some Indian expatriate-focused vendors now operate in La Cruz
- Best to ask vendors specifically: "¿Tienes especias indias?" (Do you have Indian spices?)

### Price Expectations (Realistic)
- Indian spices: 50-250 pesos/kilo (varies by import)
- Local vegetables: 20-50 pesos/kilo
- Fish: 75-120 pesos/kilo
- Bulk discounts: Ask for "¿hay descuento?"
