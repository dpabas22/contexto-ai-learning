## LESSON 2: HOSPITAL NAVIGATION

### Dialogue 1: Calling for an Appointment
```
Jasmine: "Hola, buenos días. Necesito una cita con el doctor."
ENGLISH: "Hi, good morning. I need an appointment with a doctor."

Recepcionista: "Sí, claro. ¿Cuál es tu problema o síntoma?"
ENGLISH: "Yes, of course. What's your problem or symptom?"

Jasmine: "Tengo dolor de cabeza y fiebre desde hace dos días."
ENGLISH: "I have a headache and fever for two days."

Recepcionista: "¿Prefieres doctor general o especialista?"
ENGLISH: "Do you prefer a general doctor or a specialist?"

Jasmine: "General doctor, por favor. ¿Cuándo hay disponibilidad?"
ENGLISH: "General doctor, please. When do you have availability?"

Recepcionista: "Tenemos cita mañana a las 10 de la mañana o pasado mañana a las 2 de la tarde."
ENGLISH: "We have an appointment tomorrow at 10 AM or the day after tomorrow at 2 PM."

Jasmine: "Mañana a las 10 está bien. ¿Cuál es el costo?"
ENGLISH: "Tomorrow at 10 works. What's the cost?"

Recepcionista: "Cita sin seguro, 400 pesos. Con seguro, solo copago."
ENGLISH: "Appointment without insurance, 400 pesos. With insurance, just copay."

Jasmine: "¿Cuál es el copago?"
ENGLISH: "What's the copay?"

Recepcionista: "Copago es 100 pesos. ¿Tienes seguro?"
ENGLISH: "Copay is 100 pesos. Do you have insurance?"

Jasmine: "Sí, tengo seguro con mi empresa."
ENGLISH: "Yes, I have insurance through my company."

Recepcionista: "Perfecto. Trae tu carnet de seguro mañana."
ENGLISH: "Perfect. Bring your insurance card tomorrow."
```

### Dialogue 3: Talking to Doctor/Specialist
```
Doctor: "Hola, soy el doctor García. ¿Cuál es el problema?"
ENGLISH: "Hi, I'm Doctor García. What's the problem?"

Jasmine: "Tengo dolor de cabeza, fiebre y tos desde hace tres días."
ENGLISH: "I have a headache, fever, and cough for three days."

Doctor: "¿Duele la garganta?"
ENGLISH: "Does your throat hurt?"

Jasmine: "Sí, duele mucho. Es difícil tragar."
ENGLISH: "Yes, it hurts a lot. It's difficult to swallow."

Doctor: "¿Tomas algún medicamento regularmente?"
ENGLISH: "Do you take any regular medication?"

Jasmine: "No, nada regularmente."
ENGLISH: "No, nothing regularly."

Doctor: "Voy a examinarte. Por favor, abre la boca."
ENGLISH: "I'm going to examine you. Please open your mouth."

[After examination]

Doctor: "Tienes infección de garganta. Es viral, no bacteria. Necesitas descanso y mucho líquido."
ENGLISH: "You have a throat infection. It's viral, not bacterial. You need rest and lots of fluids."

Jasmine: "¿Necesito antibióticos?"
ENGLISH: "Do I need antibiotics?"

Doctor: "No es necesario ahora. Si no mejoras en una semana, vuelve."
ENGLISH: "Not necessary now. If you don't improve in a week, come back."

Jasmine: "¿Puedo ir al trabajo mañana?"
ENGLISH: "Can I go to work tomorrow?"

Doctor: "No, necesitas descanso. Mínimo dos días."
ENGLISH: "No, you need rest. At least two days."

Jasmine: "Entendido. ¿Cuánto cuesta la consulta?"
ENGLISH: "Understood. How much is the consultation?"

Doctor: "Recepción te dirá. Lleva estas órdenes para los análisis si es necesario."
ENGLISH: "Reception will tell you. Take these orders for tests if needed."
```

### Dialogue 4: Insurance & Claims Process
```
Jasmine: "Hola, tengo preguntas sobre mi seguro y cómo hacer un reclamo."
ENGLISH: "Hi, I have questions about my insurance and how to file a claim."

Asesora de Seguros: "Claro, con gusto. ¿Qué necesitas?"
ENGLISH: "Of course, happy to help. What do you need?"

Jasmine: "Tuve una consulta hoy. ¿Cómo presento el reclamo a mi seguro?"
ENGLISH: "I had a consultation today. How do I file a claim with my insurance?"

Asesora: "Necesitas estos documentos: factura, receta del doctor, y comprobante de pago."
ENGLISH: "You need these documents: invoice, doctor's prescription, and proof of payment."

Jasmine: "¿El hospital envía directamente al seguro o yo debo enviar?"
ENGLISH: "Does the hospital send it directly to insurance or do I send it?"

Asesora: "Nosotros podemos enviarlo directamente si das autorización. Es más fácil."
ENGLISH: "We can send it directly if you give authorization. It's easier."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto tiempo tarda el reembolso?"
ENGLISH: "How long does the reimbursement take?"

Asesora: "Normalmente, 10 a 15 días si todo está en orden."
ENGLISH: "Normally, 10 to 15 days if everything is in order."

Jasmine: "¿Qué documentos necesito guardar?"
ENGLISH: "What documents do I need to keep?"

Asesora: "Guarda copia de todo: factura, receta, comprobante de pago, y la autorización del seguro."
ENGLISH: "Keep copies of everything: invoice, prescription, proof of payment, and insurance authorization."

Jasmine: "¿Hay algún formulario que debo llenar?"
ENGLISH: "Is there any form I need to fill out?"

Asesora: "Sí, aquí está el formulario de reclamo. Es simple, toma 5 minutos."
ENGLISH: "Yes, here's the claim form. It's simple, takes 5 minutes."
```

## REAL-WORLD MISSIONS

### Mission 1: Call for an Appointment (Beginner)
**Goal:** Call a hospital and request an appointment

**What to say:**
1. "Hola, necesito una cita"
2. "Tengo [symptom]"
3. "¿Cuándo hay disponibilidad?"
4. Confirm day/time and provide name

**Practice:**
- Record yourself making the call
- Focus on clear pronunciation
- List symptoms you might have

**Difficulty:** A2

### Mission 2: Check-in at Hospital (Intermediate)
**Goal:** Successfully check in for a real appointment

**What to do:**
1. Arrive 10 minutes early
2. Approach reception: "Tengo cita con el doctor ___ a las ___"
3. Provide ID and insurance card
4. Answer questions about allergies/medications
5. Wait for doctor

**Spanish phrases:**
- "Primera vez aquí" (First time here)
- "Tengo seguro" (I have insurance)
- "No soy alérgico/a" (I'm not allergic)

**Difficulty:** A2-B1

---

### Mission 3: Insurance Claim Process (Advanced)
**Goal:** File an actual insurance claim for a medical visit

**What to do:**
1. Get invoice from hospital
2. Request claim form
3. Fill out form with date/amount/doctor
4. Gather required documents
5. Submit (in person or email)
6. Track reimbursement

**Spanish to use:**
- "¿Cómo presento un reclamo?" (How do I file a claim?)
- "¿Qué documentos necesito?" (What documents do I need?)
- "¿Cuánto tiempo tarda?" (How long does it take?)

**Difficulty:** B1-B2

## CULTURAL NOTES FOR EXPATS

### Healthcare in Querétaro
- Private healthcare is reliable and accessible
- Costs are significantly lower than US/Europe
- Doctors speak English at major hospitals
- Wait times are typically short (days, not weeks)

### Insurance Considerations
- Company insurance usually covers private hospitals
- Mexican IMSS is complex for expats (requires specific visas)
- Private insurance plans available (250-500 pesos/month)
- Always ask copay amount when booking

### Specialist Appointments
- Dermatologists: Common for skin issues (Mexican sun exposure)
- Gastroenterologists: Common due to dietary adjustment
- No referrals needed for private healthcare (unlike Mexico's public system)
- Direct access to specialists

### Emergency Care
- For emergencies: Go to hospital or call ambulance
- Say "Emergencia" clearly
- Have insurance card ready
- Most hospitals have 24-hour emergency rooms

### Medication Differences
- Many medications available over-the-counter (no prescription needed)
- Antibiotics often easier to access
- Always ask pharmacist about side effects
- Mexican brand names differ from US/India

### Important Healthcare Words to Know
- "Urgencia" = Urgent/Emergency
- "Consulta" = Consultation/Appointment
- "Receta" = Prescription
- "Dosis" = Dose
- "Efectos secundarios" = Side effects

### Tips for Doctor Visits
1. Write down symptoms in Spanish before visit
2. Write down current medications
3. Bring passport/ID
4. Ask doctor to write prescriptions clearly
5. Ask about side effects and interactions
6. Keep all receipts for insurance
