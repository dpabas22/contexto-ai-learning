## LESSON 4: SCHOOL SYSTEM NAVIGATION

### Dialogue 1: Canteen & School Rules
```
Jasmine: "Hola, ¿qué servicio ofrece la cafetería de la escuela?"
ENGLISH: "Hi, what services does the school cafeteria offer?"

Encargada de Cafetería: "Ofrecemos desayuno, almuerzo, y refrigerios. ¿Qué necesitas?"
ENGLISH: "We offer breakfast, lunch, and snacks. What do you need?"

Jasmine: "¿Cuáles son las opciones de menú?"
ENGLISH: "What are the menu options?"

Encargada: "Para desayuno, tenemos quesadillas, chilaquiles, pan dulce, leche, jugo. El almuerzo cambia cada día: lunes es pollo, martes es pasta, miércoles es sopa."
ENGLISH: "For breakfast, we have quesadillas, chilaquiles, sweet bread, milk, juice. Lunch changes daily: Monday is chicken, Tuesday is pasta, Wednesday is soup."

Jasmine: "¿Cuánto cuesta el almuerzo?"
ENGLISH: "How much is lunch?"

Encargada: "30 pesos por almuerzo. El desayuno es 20 pesos. Los refrigerios son 10-15 pesos."
ENGLISH: "30 pesos for lunch. Breakfast is 20 pesos. Snacks are 10-15 pesos."

Jasmine: "¿Puedo meter dinero en una cuenta del niño o pagar cada día?"
ENGLISH: "Can I deposit money into a child's account or pay each day?"

Encargada: "Mejor es una cuenta. Depositas dinero y la maestra controla lo que come."
ENGLISH: "Better is an account. You deposit money and the teacher controls what they eat."

Jasmine: "¿Cuáles son las reglas de la escuela sobre comida?"
ENGLISH: "What are the school rules about food?"

Encargada: "No se permite traer comida de afuera. No hay dulces ni refrescos en la escuela. Es para mantener buenos hábitos de salud."
ENGLISH: "Outside food is not allowed. No candy or soft drinks at school. It's to maintain good health habits."

Jasmine: "¿Y si mi hijo tiene alergias?"
ENGLISH: "What if my child has allergies?"

Encargada: "Avísanos al inicio del año. Podemos preparar alternativas."
ENGLISH: "Tell us at the beginning of the year. We can prepare alternatives."
```


### Dialogue 4: Admin Payments & Registration
```
Jasmine: "Hola, necesito información sobre las cuotas de inscripción."
ENGLISH: "Hi, I need information about enrollment fees."

Administrador: "¿Es nuevo alumno o actual?"
ENGLISH: "Is it a new student or current?"

Jasmine: "Nuevo alumno. Es mi primer año en la escuela."
ENGLISH: "New student. It's their first year at school."

Administrador: "Para estudiantes nuevos, la cuota de inscripción es 1,500 pesos."
ENGLISH: "For new students, the enrollment fee is 1,500 pesos."

Jasmine: "¿Qué incluye eso?"
ENGLISH: "What does that include?"

Administrador: "Incluye libros, material didáctico, acceso a plataforma digital, seguro escolar."
ENGLISH: "It includes books, teaching materials, digital platform access, school insurance."

Jasmine: "¿Hay cuota mensual también?"
ENGLISH: "Is there a monthly fee too?"

Administrador: "Sí. La mensualidad es 3,000 pesos. Se paga el primer día de cada mes."
ENGLISH: "Yes. The monthly fee is 3,000 pesos. It's paid on the first of each month."

Jasmine: "¿Qué métodos de pago aceptan?"
ENGLISH: "What payment methods do you accept?"

Administrador: "Efectivo, transferencia bancaria, o tarjeta de crédito."
ENGLISH: "Cash, bank transfer, or credit card."

Jasmine: "¿Hay descuento si pago varios meses adelantado?"
ENGLISH: "Is there a discount if I pay several months in advance?"

Administrador: "Si pagas 6 meses adelantados, te damos 5% de descuento."
ENGLISH: "If you pay 6 months in advance, we give you 5% discount."

Jasmine: "¿Hay gastos adicionales?"
ENGLISH: "Are there additional expenses?"

Administrador: "Sí. Uniforme (400 pesos), libros adicionales (800 pesos), actividades (150 pesos cada una). Uniforme de educación física es 250 pesos."
ENGLISH: "Yes. Uniform (400 pesos), additional books (800 pesos), activities (150 pesos each). PE uniform is 250 pesos."

Jasmine: "¿Dónde compro el uniforme?"
ENGLISH: "Where do I buy the uniform?"

Administrador: "Hay tiendas autorizadas. Pero la mejor es en el centro comercial, tienda de uniformes escolares. Aquí está la lista."
ENGLISH: "There are authorized stores. But the best is at the shopping center, the school uniform store. Here's the list."

Jasmine: "¿Necesito todo antes de que comience la escuela?"
ENGLISH: "Do I need everything before school starts?"

Administrador: "Uniforme es obligatorio desde el primer día. Libros es importante pero puedes comprarlos en la primera semana. El resto puedes preparar gradualmente."
ENGLISH: "Uniform is mandatory from the first day. Books are important but you can buy them the first week. The rest you can prepare gradually."

Jasmine: "¿Puedo hacer un plan de pagos?"
ENGLISH: "Can I make a payment plan?"

Administrador: "Claro. Si es difícil pagar todo de una vez, podemos dividir los gastos iniciales en 2-3 pagos."
ENGLISH: "Of course. If it's hard to pay all at once, we can split the initial expenses into 2-3 payments."

Jasmine: "¿A quién le pago?"
ENGLISH: "Who do I pay?"

Administrador: "Aquí en administración. Yo proceso todos los pagos. Te daré recibo por cada pago."
ENGLISH: "Here in administration. I process all payments. I'll give you a receipt for each payment."
```

## CULTURAL NOTES FOR EXPAT PARENTS

### Mexican School System
- **Start date:** August (after summer)
- **School year:** August to June (10-11 months)
- **Holidays:** Semana Santa (spring break), Christmas, mid-year break
- **Uniforms:** Mandatory in most private schools
- **Language:** Spanish medium, but bilingual options common
- **Class size:** 25-35 students typical in private schools

### Payment Expectations
- First payment due before or during first week
- Monthly payments due on 1st of month
- Late payments may result in exclusion
- Payment plans available for genuine hardship
- Receipt ALWAYS given (keep for taxes/records)

### School Rules & Discipline
- Uniforms must be worn correctly (no modifications without permission)
- Punctuality is emphasized
- Zero-tolerance for bullying
- Parent involvement expected
- Regular communication with teachers (email/WhatsApp groups)
- Report cards every 6-8 weeks

### Food & Allergies
- School cafeterias generally healthy
- No outside food allowed (for fairness/safety)
- Allergies must be declared at enrollment
- Lunch options are basic Mexican food
- Vegetarian options usually available
- Water/juice provided; soft drinks discouraged

### Communication Methods
- WhatsApp groups (very common)
- Email for formal communication
- In-person meetings for serious issues
- Translator services NOT typically available (parents expected to manage)
- English often spoken by admin (but Spanish appreciated)

### Activity Participation
- Optional but encouraged
- Develops social skills + fitness
- Good for making friends
- Transportation provided until 5:30 PM
- After that, parent pickup required
- Cost reasonable (150-300 pesos/month)

### Director Access
- Appointments required (not walk-ins)
- Usually available 2-4 PM weekdays
- Non-emergency issues take 1-2 weeks wait
- Bring documentation if discussing grades/behavior
- Respectful communication expected
- Spanish preferable (shows respect)

### Expat Parent Tips
1. **Budget carefully:** Costs add up (uniforms, books, activities)
2. **Join parent groups:** WhatsApp groups essential for updates
3. **Attend parent meetings:** Even if in Spanish, shows commitment
4. **Learn the schedule:** Mexican holidays/breaks differ from other countries
5. **Get receipts:** Tax deductions may be available
6. **Communicate early:** Address concerns before they escalate
7. **Respect hierarchy:** Director > Teacher hierarchy is important
8. **Learn key vocabulary:** School communication happens in Spanish

### Common Challenges
- **Language barrier:** Not all staff speak English
- **Cost:** Private schools expensive for expat budgets
- **Cultural differences:** Mexican school culture differs from home
- **Payment timing:** Monthly payments can strain budgets
- **Activity coordination:** Multiple activities can be logistically complex
